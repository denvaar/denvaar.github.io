It’s a difficult watch, but if you squint hard enough there are elements of the film that can be appreciated. It’s a movie about romance, of course, but it isn’t too cliché. Instead, I think it does a good job capturing what happens when someone is in love (good and bad; rational and irrational). The two main characters are caught up in this emotion. The guy wanders around lonely city, interacting with a stray dog before he comes across the girl. The girl is hopelessly waiting for a past love. The guy, after learning of her situation, warns that she should not put so much hope into an unavailable person. As he’s pursuing her throughout the course of the film, we’re shown the irony of the relationship he’s trying to create. He’s basically walking into the same trap that he warned her about all along. Just as we’re made to think these two might actually be together, it all comes crashing down, and he’s back with the dog from the beginning. So, while it was a “romance” movie, the mood is sad and lonely rather than the nice stuff.
